A few things
worth knowing.

Clear answers before you get started.

Can I try Karafuu first?

Yes. Every new organization starts on a free trial with the full product. No card is needed to start.

Can I manage more than one property?

Yes. One organization can hold several properties. Staff only see the properties you give them access to.

Who can see my property's data?

Only members of your organization. Access is checked on every request, and each organization's data is kept separate.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Contact us to cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you paid for, with no renewal invoice.
